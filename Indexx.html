QR code safety

Before approving any QR-based transaction:

  • Check whether the screen says Pay or Collect.
  • Verify the recipient name and amount.
  • Never enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Do not trust screenshots as proof of payment.

QR decoding depends on browser support for BarcodeDetector. This feature does not make payments or automatically open decoded links.

Cyber fraud learning centre

UPI & QR scams

Fraudsters may send collect requests or fake payment screenshots. A UPI PIN authorizes payment, not receipt.

Phishing & fake links

Fake bank, delivery and government pages try to steal credentials. Verify the exact domain independently.

OTP & banking fraud

Never disclose OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords, even to someone claiming to be bank staff.

Fake job offers

Beware of registration fees, paid tasks, guaranteed income and requests to move money.

Investment scams

Guaranteed returns, pressure to invest quickly and fees to release withdrawals are serious warning signs.

Digital arrest scams

Criminals may impersonate police or officials and threaten arrest to demand money. Verify independently.

Social media impersonation

Verify urgent money requests through a separate known phone number.

SIM swap & account takeover

Contact your telecom provider if your phone suddenly loses service unexpectedly. Secure accounts through official channels.

Banking safety

  • Use unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Review account sessions.

Device safety

  • Update apps and OS.
  • Use a screen lock.
  • Never give remote access to strangers.

Emergency help — India

If money has been lost, act immediately. Contact your bank/payment provider and call financial cyber fraud helpline 1930.
  1. Contact your bank/payment provider using its verified official number.
  2. Call 1930 promptly for suspected financial cyber fraud.
  3. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
  4. Preserve transaction IDs, messages, screenshots and phone numbers.
  5. Secure compromised accounts and revoke unknown sessions.
  6. Beware of recovery agents demanding money or OTPs.

Report suspected fraud communications through Sanchar Saathi / Chakshu.
